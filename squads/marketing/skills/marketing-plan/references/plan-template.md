# Plan template

`plan/90-day.md` has these eight H2 sections, in order. Use tables for anything with rows.

## Contents

1. Executive summary
2. Strategic frame
3. Current state
4. Moves by funnel stage
5. 90-day roadmap
6. Budget
7. Measurement
8. Open decisions

## 1. Executive summary

- Three bets, one line each.
- The 90-day priorities in order, five at most.
- Day-90 success in the brand's numbers.
- What will not be done this quarter.

## 2. Strategic frame

- Market category.
- Audience and Schwartz awareness level.
- Positioning in one sentence, backed by `brand.md`.
- Market-quality gate: problem size x frequency and what it caps.
- Voice rules from `brand.md`.

## 3. Current state

- The score table from current-state-rubric.md, one row per area.
- The binding constraint (current-state-rubric.md -> Reading the scores).
- What works and stays.
- What is stuck, and the user's stated reason.

## 4. Moves by funnel stage

One table per stage, the binding constraint's stage first. Stages: Acquisition, Activation, Retention, Referral, Revenue.

| Move | Mechanism | Indicator | Owner | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

A move without a mechanism or an indicator is an experiment: give it a volume, one variable and a kill date, or cut it.

## 5. 90-day roadmap

| Stage | Weeks | Goal | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

- **Unblock** (weeks 1-2): the fix the binding constraint needs.
- **Foundation** (3-4): reusable assets: identity, templates, calendar rhythm.
- **Velocity** (5-8): the weekly calendar at `brand.md -> weekly_quota`.
- **Compound** (9-12): review the data, scale what works, start the next channel.

## 6. Budget

- Monthly budget from `brand.md`, split 70% current channels, 20% next channel, 10% experiments.
- Spend rows in `brand.md -> currency`: paid media, tools, production, AI.
- Zero budget: name the free moves that carry the quarter.
- CAC: a measured number, or `PROVISIONAL` with an entry in open decisions.

## 7. Measurement

- One north star metric for the quarter.
- Leading indicators per funnel stage, reviewed weekly.
- Kill criteria for experiments and channels: the number and the date.
- Data window and source per metric; "no new data" is a valid result.

## 8. Open decisions

Every question only the user can settle, ordered by impact, each with what it blocks and the assumption used until it is settled. CAC and price changes first.
