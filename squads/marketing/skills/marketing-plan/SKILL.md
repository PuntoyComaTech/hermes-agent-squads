# Marketing plan

Every move names its mechanism and its indicator, or it is an experiment with a kill criterion.

## When to Use

- Onboarding is approved and the user asks for the plan or a diagnosis.
- The situation changed: new data, a plateau, a new offer, or a kill criterion fired.
- The user asks for a 12-month outlook: same method, quarters instead of weeks.

## Procedure

1. **Gather.** Read `brand.md`, `preferences.md`, `research/`, `data/` and any previous plan. Extract offer and prices, audience and awareness level, channels and results, budget, key dates, and past work worth keeping. List every missing number as an open decision.
2. **Score** each area per [current-state-rubric.md](references/current-state-rubric.md) and name the binding constraint (rubric -> Reading the scores).
3. **Market-quality gate.** Rate the core problem on size (small to large) and frequency (rare to constant). A small or rare problem caps achievable CAC; record it in open decisions.
4. **Write** `plan/90-day.md` with the eight sections of [plan-template.md](references/plan-template.md).
5. **Review with the user** one section at a time; keep the approved text in the file. The plan is approved only when the user says so.
6. **Version.** `plan/progress.md` records phase, section and version. Never overwrite an approved plan: open v<N+1> and state what changed and why.
7. **Feed the calendar.** Every calendar piece serves a roadmap stage, or it is an experiment with a kill date, or it is cut.

## Pitfalls

- Generic lines ("build a community", "improve SEO") with no move, owner or indicator.
- Spend beyond the monthly budget in `brand.md`.
- Easy channels sequenced before the binding constraint.
- Tactics the user cannot run at current size or headcount.

## Verification

- Every move has a mechanism, an indicator and an owner; every experiment has a kill criterion.
- The plan shows the current-state scores with reasons and names the binding constraint.
- Budget rows add up to the monthly budget in `brand.md`.
- Open decisions list every unconfirmed number, CAC first.
- The plan uses `brand.md -> language` and `currency`.
