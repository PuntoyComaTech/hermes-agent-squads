## 1. Audience stage

- **Awareness level** (Schwartz): unaware, problem-aware, solution-aware, product-aware, most aware. Write to the next level only.
- **Market sophistication** (Schwartz): in a claim-weary market, lead with the mechanism; in a new market, lead with the claim.
- **Curse of knowledge**: write the first sentence for someone who has never heard of the offer.

## 2. Message mechanisms

- **Hook, story, offer** (Brunson).
- **Value equation** (Hormozi): Value = (Dream Outcome x Perceived Likelihood of Achievement) / (Time Delay x Effort and Sacrifice). Raise likelihood (proof, guarantee) before discounting.
- **Specificity**: "15 minutes", not "fast".
- **Single call to action**.
- **Friction reduction**: fewer fields, taps and choices.
- **Social proof**: only verified proof points with permission.
- **Loss aversion**: only with a real loss.
- **Anchoring**.
- **Scarcity**: only what the brand can prove.
- **Goal gradient**.
- **Peak-end rule**.
- **Mere exposure**: one signal repeated on a schedule.
- **Pratfall effect**: one small, true admission.
- **Distinctive brand assets** (Sharp).
- **Commitment and consistency** (Cialdini).
- **Framing**: only true frames.
- **Decoy effect**: every option must be one the brand sells.
- **Reciprocity** (Cialdini): give something usable before the ask, once.

## 3. Offer and pricing treatments

- **Charm vs. round pricing**: charm prices read as a deal, round prices as premium; match `brand.md` positioning.
- **Rule of 100**: under 100, show a percentage off; over 100, an absolute amount.
- **Good-better-best tiers**.
- **Mental accounting**: cost per use or per month, when the math is exact.
- **Bundles**: only items bought together already.
- **Risk reversal** (trial, guarantee): only a guarantee the brand will honor.

## 4. Delivery and design

- **Hick's law**: one visible action per screen or slide.
- **Salience**: the offer is the highest-contrast element.
- **Layout**: `brand-contract -> Formats`.
- **Repetition with variation**: the same signal in new formats, not a republished piece.
