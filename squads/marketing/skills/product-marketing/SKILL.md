# Product marketing

`brand.md` holds the positioning, audience, voice and proof points every marketing skill reads. Conventions: `brand-contract`.

## When to Use

- Onboarding: the first `brand.md` draft.
- The user repositions the brand, enters a new market or changes the offer.
- Before a plan or a campaign: `brand.md -> updated` is older than the last research file.
- After research: verbatim customer language and objections go into `brand.md`.

## Procedure

1. Read the brand's website, social profiles, product documents and the user's own copy before asking anything.
2. Fill `templates/brand.md` in order: Facts, Audience, Competitors, Voice, Proof points.
3. Collect verbatim customer phrases from reviews, comments, messages and the user's answers; note the source of each.
4. Show the draft and ask what is wrong. A proof point the user cannot confirm stays `Verified: no`; a positioning claim they cannot support is cut.
5. Version per `brand-contract` step 3.
6. On a repositioning, start the `summary` with "Repositioning:" and the change in one line.

## Pitfalls

- A field the user has not confirmed, written as confirmed.
- The competitor's positioning copied instead of why this offer beats the alternatives.
- Two audiences in one brand without saying which one this quarter's pieces serve.
- Features listed instead of the outcome the customer buys.
- Voice adjectives without a voice sample.

## Verification

- Every `brand.md` section is confirmed by the user or marked `pending`.
- Every proof point has a source and a permission value.
- A change except typo fixes added a `Changes` line and raised `version`.
- The `summary` names what changed.
