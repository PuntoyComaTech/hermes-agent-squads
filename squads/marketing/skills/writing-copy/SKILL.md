# Writing copy

The idea and the words, one channel at a time. You have no web access: the brief, `brand.md`, `preferences.md` and `research/` are your only inputs. Conventions: `brand-contract`.

## When to Use

- A `copy` stage card asks for `concept.md` and `copy.md` for a piece.
- The piece needs two or three campaign territories to choose from.
- A `copy-r<N>` or `copy-c<N>` card quotes the requested change.

## Procedure

1. Read the card body, `brief.md`, `brand.md` and `preferences.md`. If the brief or a brand fact is unclear, block per `brand-contract` step 7.
2. List at least five ideas, discard the first three, keep the least obvious one that fits the brief.
3. Write `concept.md`: the idea in one sentence, the mechanism in one line (`marketing-psychology`), and the art direction per `brand-contract -> Formats`. For video, a storyboard table: scene, seconds, visual, on-screen text, voice-over.
4. Write `copy.md` per channel, one message and one call to action:
   - **Post, story**: the hook in the first line; length, hashtags and emojis per `brand.md -> Voice`.
   - **Carousel**: one idea per slide, 6-10 slides, the last slide holds the call to action.
   - **Ad**: three hook variants and two calls to action, within the placement's character limits.
   - **Email**: subject line of 45 characters or fewer, preheader, one goal, plain-text version.
   - **Landing**: promise, problem, mechanism, proof, offer, objections, FAQ, one call to action.
   - **Article**: match the search intent, the direct answer in the first paragraph, one H2 per subtopic.
   - **Script**: the hook in the first 2 s, beats timed at 150 words per minute, on-screen text of 6 words or fewer, subtitles, the call to action at the end.
5. Write alt text for every image. Add UTM parameters to every outbound link (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`, `utm_content` = piece id); never to internal links.
6. Run the copy through `humanizer` and delete machine tells: "in today's fast-paced world", "it is worth noting", "unlock", "dive in", "not only... but also", stacked dashes, bold on every phrase, and their equivalents in the brand's language.
7. In `sources.md`, name the `brand.md` proof point behind every claim and the source of every external fact or phrase.
8. Hand off per `brand-contract` step 6, with the mechanism in the `summary`.

## Pitfalls

- The same copy pasted across networks.
- Manufactured urgency or scarcity, or a competitor comparison that is not factual.
- Changing what the user asked to keep, or the call to action, to fit a line.
- Copy that sounds like the category instead of the brand's voice samples.

## Verification

- One message and one call to action per channel; the hook is in the first line (video: first 2 s).
- Every claim maps to a verified `brand.md` proof point, with permission where a person is quoted.
- Copy matches the voice samples, vocabulary and form of address in `brand.md`.
- `concept.md`, `copy.md` and `sources.md` exist in the piece folder.
