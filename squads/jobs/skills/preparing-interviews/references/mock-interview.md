# Mock interview protocol

A mock interview runs in the conversation with the user, after the pack exists. The manager runs it in chat, or the
writer runs it when the user asks for it in a card. It is practice against the record: the answers must match the paper.

## Contents

- Setup
- Round order
- Feedback after each answer
- Closing

## Setup

1. Confirm the opening and the stage. Load `interview.md`. Never practise from memory of the opening.
2. Ask which language to practise in, and whether the user wants a hard round (curveballs) or a gentle one.
3. Read `profile.md` behavioral and writing_style. Practise in the user's register, not a generic ideal.

## Round order

1. Warm-up: "Tell me about yourself." The answer is under two minutes and cites the same claims as the CV.
2. Role-specific questions: two or three from the likely list, the ones most likely to be asked.
3. One or two behavioural questions tied to the posting's competencies. Use the STAR mapping.
4. One tough question or a curveball: a gap, a tenure question, or a question the pack did not prepare.
5. "Do you have questions for us?" Check that the question list is used, not a generic one.

Ask one question at a time and wait for the answer. Do not answer for the user.

## Feedback after each answer

Give three short points, in this order:

- What worked: the phrase or number that landed.
- What to sharpen: one change, such as a shorter first sentence, the result stated, or the scope named.
- Which story from the pack would have served better, when another one fits.

Check every claim the user makes against `achievements.yaml`. A claim that is not on the paper is flagged: "That is not in
your CV or in the record. Keep it out of the room, or add it to the record first, with your confirmation." Never accept
a title or a level stronger than the record.

## Closing

- Summarise in three lines: two strengths, two things to rehearse, and the question the user is least ready for.
- Offer to save notes. Write nothing to the project folder unless the user asks; when asked, append to `interview.md`
  under a dated "Mock round" heading.
- Remind the user to log the stage after the interview, so the next round's pack learns from it.
